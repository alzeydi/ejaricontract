DEWA transfer to a new apartment:
Move-to, step by step

Updated October 2026 · checked against DEWA’s Move-to service page · ⏱ Live within 15 working hours

Moving apartments in Dubai? DEWA has a dedicated service for it: Move-to (“Transfer of Electricity/Water”). One request in the DEWA app switches the supply off at your old home and on at the new one, and — the part most guides still get wrong — your security deposit moves with you. You don’t pay a fresh AED 2,000 and wait for a refund; you only top up the difference if the new home needs a bigger deposit (flat → villa). Fees for a standard apartment meter come to AED 275 for both legs, and the new connection goes live within 15 working hours. This guide covers the exact inputs DEWA asks for, the fees line by line, the timing rules (no Sunday disconnections) and the cases where you still need the old Move Out + Move In route.

i

Before you start: four things DEWA asks for

The Move-to form is linked to Dubai Land Department records, so it checks the new home against its Ejari before it lets you submit. Have these ready:
  • Contract account number of your current home — top of any DEWA bill or in the app.
  • Move-out date for the old home (and a time slot).
  • 9-digit premises number of the new home — printed on its Ejari certificate as “Premises No. (DEWA)”. Can’t find it? See where to find the DEWA premises number.
  • Ejari number of the new home — a new Ejari is mandatory. Not registered yet? Do that first: how to register Ejari.
Clear any unpaid DEWA balance on the current home first — Move-to is refused while dues are outstanding.
1

Open Move-to in the DEWA app or website

Sign in to the DEWA Smart App or dewa.gov.ae with UAE PASS or your DEWA ID and choose Move-to (Transfer of Electricity/Water). Individuals can also use the Dubai Now app. Both channels are open 24/7. 📱 App or website, 24/7
2

Enter the old account and the new home

Select the current contract account, set the move-out date, then enter the new home’s premises number and Ejari number. DEWA verifies both against Dubai Land Department records. Moves must stay in the same category — residential to residential, or commercial to commercial.
If the form says the new premises still has an active account, the previous occupant hasn’t moved out. Call DEWA Customer Care on 04 601 9999 with your Ejari certificate.
3

Pay the fees — and a deposit top-up only if needed

Your existing security deposit is transferred to the new account. You pay a difference only when the new home requires a larger deposit, for example moving from a flat (AED 2,000) to a villa (AED 4,000).
Deactivation at the old home (small meter)AED 112.50
Knowledge + innovation fees (deactivation)AED 20
Activation at the new home (small meter)AED 112.50
Registration + knowledge + innovation fees (activation)AED 30
Total, standard apartmentAED 275
Large meters (per leg, instead of AED 112.50)AED 300
Deposit: flat / villaAED 2,000 / AED 4,000 — carried over

✓

New home live within 15 working hours

Electricity and water at the new home are connected within 15 working hours of payment. The old home is disconnected within 24 working hours of the date and time you chose, and the final bill for it arrives by email and SMS within another 24 working hours. DEWA’s working hours are Monday to Saturday, 7:00 am – 8:00 pm, and move-out disconnections don’t happen on Sundays or public holidays. ⚡ Submit a day or two before the move
i

Timing it so the lights never go off

Because “15 working hours” skips nights and Sundays, a request paid on Saturday evening can take until Monday. Submit Move-to at least one working day before you pick up the keys, and set the move-out date for the old home on or just after your handover day so you aren’t paying for a flat you’ve left. Then pay the old home’s final bill as soon as it arrives by email and SMS.
i

When to use Move Out + Move In instead

Move-to covers the normal case — one Dubai home to another in the same category. Use the separate services when it doesn’t fit:
  • Leaving Dubai, or moving into a home with bills included — submit Move Out only (AED 125 small meter + AED 20 fees). The deposit is offset against the final bill and the balance refunded to your IBAN.
  • Residential ↔ commercial — Move-to doesn’t cross categories, so close one account and open the other.
  • First DEWA account in Dubai — nothing to transfer; follow the first-time DEWA activation guide (AED 155 in fees plus the deposit).
  • Renewing the lease on the same flat — DEWA isn’t involved at all. Your account and deposit stay as they are; just renew your Ejari.
Moving within the same building still counts as a move: every unit has its own premises number, so apartment 402 → 605 is a Move-to like any other.
i

Two deposits, two refunds — don't mix them up

When you change apartments there are two separate deposits in play. The DEWA deposit (AED 2,000 flat / AED 4,000 villa) follows you to the new home with Move-to. The rental security deposit — typically 5% of annual rent for an unfurnished home, 10% furnished — is held by your old landlord and refunded after the handover inspection, and this one is where disputes happen. Close the old tenancy properly too: cancel the old Ejari (free) so the next tenant can register.
If your landlord withholds the rental deposit without justification, see how to get your security deposit back or file a case at the Rental Dispute Centre.

Frequently asked questions

How do I transfer DEWA to a new apartment?

Use DEWA’s Move-to service in the DEWA app, on dewa.gov.ae or in Dubai Now. Enter your current contract account number, the move-out date, and the new home’s premises number and Ejari number, then pay the fees. The new home is connected within 15 working hours and the old one is disconnected on the date you chose.

Does my DEWA deposit transfer to the new apartment?

Yes. With Move-to, the security deposit on your current account is transferred to the new one, so you don’t pay a fresh deposit or apply for a refund. You only pay the difference if the new home needs a larger deposit — for example AED 2,000 more when moving from a flat to a villa.

How much does it cost to transfer DEWA?

For standard (small) meters DEWA lists AED 112.50 to deactivate the old home plus AED 20 in knowledge and innovation fees, and AED 112.50 to activate the new home plus AED 30 in registration, knowledge and innovation fees — AED 275 in total. Large meters are AED 300 per leg instead of AED 112.50.

How long does DEWA take to connect the new apartment?

Within 15 working hours of payment. DEWA counts working hours as Monday to Saturday, 7:00 am to 8:00 pm, so a request paid late on Saturday may not be live until Monday. Submit at least one working day before you move in.

Can I still do Move Out and Move In separately?

Yes. Move Out costs AED 125 for a small meter plus AED 20 in fees and refunds the deposit balance to your IBAN after the final bill; Move In costs AED 155 in fees plus a new deposit. It makes sense when you are leaving Dubai or switching between residential and commercial premises; for a normal flat-to-flat move, Move-to is cheaper and keeps your deposit.
