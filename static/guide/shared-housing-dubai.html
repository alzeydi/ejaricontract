Dubai shared housing law 2026:
permits, fines and what tenants can do

بالعربية · Updated October 2026 · Law No. 4 of 2026 · Dubai Media Office and Dubai Municipality Circular 1-3-1/2026

Is bed space or room-sharing still legal in Dubai? Only in buildings with a shared-housing permit. Law No. 4 of 2026 — in force since 8 September 2026 — requires a permit for any shared-housing unit, bans tenants from subletting any part of a unit and fines violators AED 500 to AED 500,000. This guide sums up the rules from the official announcement and the Municipality circular, and what they mean for tenants and owners.

i

The short answer: Law No. 4 of 2026 is in force since 8 September 2026

Law No. (4) of 2026 regulates the management and occupancy of shared housing in Dubai. Dubai Municipality’s Circular 1-3-1 of 2026 (dated 7 September 2026) confirms it took effect on 8 September 2026. In short: nobody may allocate a unit or building to shared housing without a permit, the permit covers a whole building or villa, and tenants are not allowed to sublease any part of a unit.
Who needs a permitOwners and establishments that lease or manage units for shared housing. Collective labour accommodation is outside the law.
Permit lengthOne year, renewable for similar periods; owners may ask for two years. Renewal must be filed at least 30 days before expiry.
FinesAED 500 to AED 500,000; a repeat within one year doubles the fine, up to AED 1 million.
Existing shared housingOne year from 8 September 2026 to comply (building and change-of-use violations are not covered by the grace period).
Sources: Dubai Media Office announcement of 11 March 2026 and Dubai Municipality Circular 1-3-1/2026. Technical standards (space per occupant, fire safety, sanitary ratios) sit in Municipality guidance that this page does not restate — check the current Dubai Building Platform guide before relying on any number you read in the press.
1

Permit rules: whole building, one category

Under the Municipality circular, which applies the law to owners, engineering consultants and contractors:
  • The entire building or villa must be designated for shared housing — part of a building cannot be.
  • One building holds one category only: either individuals or families. The two cannot be mixed.
  • The owner applies through an engineering consultant on the Dubai Building Platform; the permit is issued when the completion certificate is issued, and its data goes to the Dubai Land Department so tenancy contracts can be issued to occupants.
  • Shared housing is allowed only in zones authorised by the Municipality and other competent bodies.
2

Subletting beds or rooms is banned

The law allows three routes: the owner leases directly, an establishment manages the unit for the owner, or an establishment leases from the owner in order to sublet. In every case only the owner or an authorised establishment may lease the shared-housing unit. In the law’s words, tenants or other parties may not sublease any part of the unit. That makes the classic “main tenant sublets bed spaces” model non-compliant. For the ordinary sublease rules that apply outside shared housing, see subletting in Dubai.

Penalties for owners and operators

  • Fines of AED 500 to AED 500,000; a repeat violation within a year doubles the fine, capped at AED 1 million.
  • The Dubai Land Department may suspend the activity for up to six months, cancel the permit, revoke the commercial licence, disconnect public services until the violation is fixed, or order eviction of non-compliant units.
  • Disputes under the law go exclusively to the Rental Disputes Centre.
  • Existing owners and establishments have one year to comply; the Director General of Dubai Municipality may grant a single extension.
i

Tenant checklist: before you pay for a bed space or room

  • Ask for the shared-housing permit of the building and check that the landlord is the owner or an authorised establishment, not another tenant.
  • Insist on a proper tenancy contract — the permit data is meant to let DLD issue contracts to occupants. A cash receipt from a main tenant is not protection.
  • Remember how Ejari works: DLD’s FAQ says a co-occupant does not get an Ejari certificate (co-occupants can be added in the Dubai REST app), and one Ejari cannot be made under two tenant names. See Ejari registration.
  • Keep every payment traceable and ask for the deposit terms in writing; see security deposit refund if it is withheld.
  • If the unit is shut down or you are told to leave, the eviction notice rules and the RDC route apply.

What owners should do now

  • Decide per building whether it will be shared housing at all — and if so, for individuals or families.
  • Hire an engineering consultant and file on the Dubai Building Platform; do not leave it to the end of the one-year window.
  • Fix any building or change-of-use violation first: the grace year does not cover them.
  • Diarise the permit renewal 30 days before expiry.

Frequently asked questions

What is the shared housing law in Dubai?

Law No. (4) of 2026 regulates the management and occupancy of shared housing in Dubai. It requires a permit for any unit or building allocated to shared housing, bans subletting by tenants and sets fines of AED 500 to AED 500,000.

When did the shared housing law start in Dubai?

Dubai Municipality Circular 1-3-1 of 2026, dated 7 September 2026, states the law took effect on 8 September 2026. Existing shared housing has one year from that date to comply.

Is bed space legal in Dubai?

Only inside a building that holds a shared-housing permit and is leased by the owner or an authorised establishment. A tenant subletting a bed space or room in a normal rental unit breaks the law, which bans tenants from subleasing any part of a unit.

What are the fines under the Dubai shared housing law?

AED 500 to AED 500,000. A repeat violation within one year doubles the fine, up to AED 1 million. Dubai Land Department can also suspend the activity for up to six months or cancel the permit.

Can families and bachelors share one building?

No. Under the Municipality circular a shared-housing building or villa may serve only one category — individuals or families — and the two cannot be combined in the same building.

Do I get an Ejari certificate for a bed space?

DLD’s FAQ says there is no Ejari certificate for a co-occupant, and an Ejari cannot be registered under two tenant names. In permitted shared housing the permit data lets DLD issue tenancy contracts to occupants.

Where do shared housing disputes go?

The Dubai Rental Disputes Centre has exclusive jurisdiction over disputes arising under the law and its related decisions.
