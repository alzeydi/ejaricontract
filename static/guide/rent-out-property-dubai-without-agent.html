Rent out your property in Dubai without an agent
5 steps from title deed to Ejari

Updated November 2026 · Law 26/2007 Arts. 4, 7, 9, 12, 14–16, 20, 21, 24, 25, 34 as amended by Law 33/2008 · checked against dlp.dubai.gov.ae

Can you rent out a Dubai property without an agent? Yes. The rental law regulates the landlord and the tenant directly: you need a written lease with the contents Article 4 lists and an Ejari registration (AED __EJARI_FEE_ONLINE__ online). Here is the full do-it-yourself flow and the rules that still apply.

i

The short answer: yes — the law regulates landlord and tenant, not brokers

Dubai’s rental law (Law 26/2007, as amended by Law 33/2008) places its duties on the Landlord and the Tenant: a written lease with set contents (Art. 4(1)) and registration of every lease with RERA, which is what Ejari is (Art. 4(2)). Nothing in it requires a broker. Doing it yourself means you handle the five things an agent normally does:
1. Prove you can let the unitTitle deed in your name — tenants can verify it free with DLD
2. Agree the termsRent, payment dates, deposit, term, maintenance, extra clauses
3. Sign the DLD unified contractMust state property, purpose, term, rent, payment method; owner’s name if you are not the owner (Art. 4(1))
4. Register EjariAED __EJARI_FEE_ONLINE__ online · AED __EJARI_FEE_TYPING__ at a trustee centre
5. Hand over and keep recordsCondition report, keys, receipts, deposit terms (Arts. 15, 20, 21)
1

Get your ownership papers ready

A careful tenant will check that the person signing is the owner, or that the contract names the owner where the landlord is someone else — amended Article 4(1) requires the owner’s name in that case. Have your title deed number to hand; tenants can confirm it free and instantly in DLD’s Verify Title Deed service, which makes a direct deal without an agent easier to trust.
2

Set the rent, cheques and deposit

Write the rent into the contract — Article 9 (as amended) requires it. Agree payment dates: if you do not, Article 12 makes the rent payable in four equal instalments in advance. You may take a security deposit to cover damage at the end of the lease, but must refund it or the balance at expiry (Art. 20). Rent increases at renewal are capped by Decree 43/2013 — check the number with our RERA rent calculator before you quote a renewal rent.
3

Fill and sign the DLD unified tenancy contract

Use the official DLD unified tenancy contract: it has the fields Article 4 needs and five lines for additional terms. Anything that does not fit goes into a signed addendum (maintenance split, early-exit terms, parking, pets). Our tenancy contract guide has the free PDF/Word and a filled sample.
4

Register Ejari — either side can file

Every lease must be registered with RERA (amended Art. 4(2)); that registration is Ejari. Filing yourself in Dubai REST or on the DLD website costs AED __EJARI_FEE_ONLINE__; a trustee (typing) centre charges AED __EJARI_FEE_TYPING__ because of a higher service-partner fee (fee breakdown). The law does not say who pays — agree it in the contract. Step-by-step: Ejari registration. Without Ejari the tenant cannot open a DEWA account in their name, so register before move-in.
5

Hand over in good condition and document it

Article 15 obliges the landlord to hand the property over in a condition that lets the tenant fully use it. Do a dated photo condition report signed by both sides — it is what decides deposit deductions at the end, because the tenant must return the unit in the same condition except for ordinary wear and tear (Art. 21). During the lease, maintenance is the landlord’s unless the contract says otherwise (Art. 16) — see who pays for maintenance.

Rules that apply with or without an agent

90 days’ notice before expiry to change rent or terms at renewal (amended Art. 14), within the Decree 43/2013 cap; no unilateral termination during the term (Art. 7); eviction only on the grounds and notices in Article 25 (12-month notice rules); and never cutting services or disturbing the tenant (Art. 34 — what that covers). The tenant may not sublet without your written consent (Art. 24).

Frequently asked questions

Is it legal to rent out my property in Dubai without a real estate agent?

Yes. Law 26/2007 (as amended by Law 33/2008) sets duties for the landlord and the tenant — a written lease with defined contents and registration with RERA through Ejari. It does not require the deal to go through a broker.

Who registers Ejari when there is no agent?

Either the landlord or the tenant can file it, online in Dubai REST or the DLD website (AED __EJARI_FEE_ONLINE__) or at a trustee centre (AED __EJARI_FEE_TYPING__). The law does not say who pays; agree it in the contract.

What must the tenancy contract include?

Amended Article 4(1): a description of the property that leaves no room for uncertainty, the purpose of the lease, the term, the rent and how it is paid, and the owner’s name if the landlord is not the owner. The DLD unified tenancy contract has fields for all of these.

How many cheques can I ask for?

The number of cheques is agreed between you. If the contract sets no payment dates, Article 12 says rent is payable annually in four equal instalments paid in advance.

Can I keep the tenant’s security deposit?

Only to cover maintenance of the property at the end of the lease. Article 20 requires the landlord to refund the deposit or its remainder at expiry; disputes go to the Rental Disputes Centre.

How much notice do I need to raise the rent at renewal?

At least 90 days before the lease expires (amended Article 14), and the increase must stay within the Decree 43/2013 cap — use the RERA rent calculator to check the allowed percentage.

Do I need a power of attorney if someone else signs for me?

If a relative or manager signs instead of the owner, the tenant and DLD will expect proof of their authority, and the contract must name the owner where the landlord is not the owner (Article 4(1)). Ask DLD or a notary about the exact form required.
