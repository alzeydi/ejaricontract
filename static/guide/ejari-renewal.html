How to renew Ejari
in Dubai — step by step

Updated July 2026 · ⏱ 10 min online · Fee: AED 122.50 · العربية

How do I renew my Ejari? Sign the renewed tenancy contract with your landlord, gather your documents, submit them through the Renew Ejari service on the Dubai REST app and pay AED 122.50 — the renewed certificate is issued the same day. In short: 1) sign the renewed contract → 2) gather documents (Emirates ID, previous Ejari, Title Deed) → 3) open Dubai REST → Renew Ejari → 4) pay AED 122.50. Ejari must be renewed every time your tenancy contract renews, even with the same landlord at the same address. This guide covers the 2026 fees, deadlines, the practical grace period and exactly what stops working if you renew late.

1

Sign the renewed tenancy contract

Before you can renew Ejari, both parties must sign a renewed tenancy contract in the official DLD Unified Tenancy Contract format with the new dates — see our Dubai tenancy contract guide for the required fields and standard terms. The new start date should be the day after the old contract ends, so there is no gap in registration.

The agreed annual rent must respect the caps in Decree 43 of 2013: if your current rent is within 10% of the market average, no increase is allowed; permitted increases scale from 5% up to a maximum of 20% only when the rent sits far below market. Check your property in the official RERA rental index calculator before signing. Any increase also requires 90 days written notice before expiry — an out-of-cap or un-notified increase can be challenged at the Rental Dispute Centre.

3

Renew Ejari on the Dubai REST app

The Dubai REST app is the Dubai Land Department's official platform and the cheapest, fastest way to renew (see the official Dubai REST page). Here is the exact in-app path:
  1. Download or open Dubai REST (iOS or Android) — or use the DLD website on desktop.
  2. Log in with UAE Pass.
  3. Go to Services → Real Estate Services → Ejari → Renew Ejari.
  4. Select the Ejari certificate being renewed from your list of active registrations.
  5. Upload the renewed contract, confirm the pre-filled details, and pay.
📱 Dubai REST app recommended
The app shows all your active Ejari certificates. Select the one being renewed — the system pre-fills the lessor, tenant, and property details from the previous record, which is why renewal is faster than first-time registration.
4

Upload the new contract and verify details

Upload the renewed contract PDF and check every pre-filled field. Pay attention to the contract dates, annual rent, and mode of payment — these change at renewal even when other fields don't.
If anything in the property section needs to change (e.g. an updated DEWA Premises Number after a building renumbering), edit it now. After submission, only the dates and rent can be amended — property fields are locked.
5

When to renew Ejari — the deadline

Renew as soon as the renewed contract starts — that is the intended deadline, because your old Ejari certificate expires the day the previous contract ends. The Dubai Land Department does not publish a countdown or send reminders; the responsibility sits with the tenant (or the landlord, if your contract says so).

In practice, aim to complete the renewal within 30 days of the new term starting. Since the process takes about 10 minutes online and the certificate arrives the same day, there is rarely a good reason to wait. Renewing on day one means your registration never lapses and every service that checks Ejari — visas, DEWA, schools — keeps working without questions.
i

The Ejari renewal grace period, explained

There is no official grace period written into the rules — but there is a practical one. Nothing automatically happens the day your Ejari expires; the certificate simply shows as expired in the system. Most tenants have roughly a 30-day window before the expiry starts to bite, because that's typically when the next Ejari-dependent transaction comes up.

What breaks while your Ejari is expired:
  • GDRFA visa applications — new or renewed residence visas for you and your family are put on hold
  • DEWA changes — Move In, Move Out and name changes are refused until the registration is active
  • School registration — Dubai schools ask for a valid Ejari as proof of residence
  • RDC filings — you cannot open a rental case without an active registration
Treat the 30 days as a safety net, not a target. The renewal itself takes 10 minutes — inside or outside the window, the process and the AED 122.50 fee are identical.
⚠️

Is there a fine for late Ejari renewal?

No — there is no fixed, published fine for renewing Ejari late, and the fee does not increase. The penalty is indirect: an expired Ejari silently blocks government services until you renew.
  • Visa renewals for tenant and family — GDRFA requires an active Ejari
  • RDC case filings — you cannot file at the Rental Dispute Centre without active registration, which matters if a dispute is brewing
  • DEWA service changes — Move In, Move Out, and name changes all require valid Ejari
  • School and parking permits issued by Dubai authorities often require Ejari proof
If your Ejari has already expired, sign the renewed contract first, then run the renewal exactly as described above — there is no separate "late renewal" path and no extra charge. The new certificate covers the new term going forward.

Frequently asked questions

How do I renew my Ejari?

Sign the renewed tenancy contract with your landlord, open the Dubai REST app, choose Renew Ejari, upload the signed contract and pay AED 122.50. The renewed certificate is issued the same day, usually within minutes.

How much does Ejari renewal cost in 2026?

AED 122.50 including VAT when you renew online through the Dubai REST app. At a typing centre expect AED 215–220. There is no discount for renewal — it costs exactly the same as a new Ejari registration.

What documents are required for Ejari renewal?

You need the renewed signed tenancy contract in DLD format, your Emirates ID, the previous Ejari certificate, a copy of the Title Deed and a recent DEWA bill. The landlord's Emirates ID or passport copy is also needed, although most fields are pre-filled from the previous registration.

Is there a fine for late Ejari renewal?

No fixed fine is published for renewing Ejari late. The real cost is what an expired Ejari blocks: GDRFA visa renewals, DEWA account changes, school registration and filing a case at the Rental Dispute Centre all require an active certificate.

What is the grace period for Ejari renewal?

There is no official grace period, but in practice you have about 30 days after the old contract expires before problems surface. During any gap your Ejari shows as expired, so renew as soon as the renewed contract starts to keep visas, DEWA and other services running.

How do I renew Ejari on the Dubai REST app?

Open Dubai REST, log in with UAE Pass, then go to Services, Real Estate Services, Ejari, Renew Ejari. Select your existing certificate, upload the renewed signed contract, confirm the pre-filled details and pay AED 122.50 by card. The new certificate arrives by email the same day.

Can I renew Ejari online?

Yes. The whole renewal is done online through the Dubai REST app or the Dubai Land Department website — no office visit is needed. Online renewal costs AED 122.50 versus AED 215–220 at a typing centre and takes about 10 minutes.

Do I need to renew Ejari every year?

Yes, Ejari must be renewed every time your tenancy contract renews, even with the same landlord at the same address. The certificate expires with the contract, so a standard annual contract means an annual Ejari renewal.

Can the landlord increase rent at Ejari renewal?

Only within the caps of Decree 43 of 2013 and the RERA rental index, and only with 90 days written notice before the contract expires. If your rent is within 10% of the market average, no increase is allowed at all.
