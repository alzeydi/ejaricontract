Ejari cost in 2026: AED 177.75,
and where every dirham goes

Updated August 2026 · AED 177.75 online · AED 219.75 at a trustee centre · Cancellation free

Ejari costs AED 177.75 if you file it yourself in the Dubai REST app or on the DLD portal, and AED 219.75 at a trustee or typing centre. The government portion — AED 120 — is identical on both routes; the entire difference is the service-partner fee and the VAT charged on it. Anything above AED 219.75 is somebody’s margin, and you are entitled to see it as a separate line.

1

The exact 2026 fee, line by line

Filed yourself in Dubai REST or on the DLD portal:
Tenancy contract registrationAED 100.00
Knowledge feeAED 10.00
Innovation feeAED 10.00
Service-partner feeAED 55.00
VAT (5% on the service-partner fee)AED 2.75
TotalAED 177.75
The AED 120 of government charges is fixed and identical on every route. Only the service-partner fee moves — which is why the channel you choose, not the emirate or the rent, decides what you pay.
2

The same registration at a trustee centre

Tenancy contract registrationAED 100.00
Knowledge feeAED 10.00
Innovation feeAED 10.00
Service-partner feeAED 95.00
VAT (5% on the service-partner fee)AED 4.75
TotalAED 219.75
You are paying AED 42 for a clerk to check the documents and file them. That is often worth it: the most common rejection reason is a contract that does not match the Title Deed, and a trustee-centre clerk catches it before it is submitted.
If your scans are clean and the contract already mirrors the Title Deed, the AED 42 buys nothing you cannot do in ten minutes yourself.

Every channel compared, with the markup shown

Dubai REST app / DLD portalAED 177.75 — government fee only, no markup. Certificate usually the same day
Trustee / typing centreAED 219.75 — government fee only, higher partner tier. Issued while you wait
Online filing servicesAED 250–350 — AED 177.75 plus roughly AED 75–175 margin
Real-estate agentAED 300–500 — AED 177.75 plus roughly AED 120–320 margin
Property-management companyAED 400–500, sometimes bundled into a management fee
A provider charging more than AED 219.75 is charging a service fee, and that is legitimate — as long as they will show you the government fee and their fee on separate lines. If they will not split it, or will not quote at all until you email an Emirates ID and a signed contract, that is the signal to walk. See Ejari help.

What the Ejari fee does not cover

Ejari is one of the smaller numbers in a Dubai move-in. The realistic total on an AED 85,000 one-bedroom looks like this:
Ejari registrationAED 177.75
Agency commission (typically 5% of annual rent)≈ AED 4,250
Security deposit (5% unfurnished / 10% furnished)AED 4,250 – 8,500 — refundable, if you document it
DEWA connection depositAED 2,000 apartment / AED 4,000 villa — refundable
DEWA activation & registration charges≈ AED 130 – 180 — DEWA activation
Tenancy contract preparationAED 0 with the free DLD template, or AED 15 auto-filled from your documents
Deposits come back; commission and fees do not. The line most people lose money on is the security deposit, not the registration fee — photograph everything on move-in day.

Frequently asked questions

How much does Ejari cost in 2026?

AED 177.75 if you file it yourself in the Dubai REST app or on the DLD portal. That is AED 100 registration, AED 10 knowledge fee, AED 10 innovation fee, AED 55 service-partner fee and AED 2.75 VAT. At a trustee or typing centre the same registration costs AED 219.75.

Why is Ejari more expensive at a typing centre?

The government portion is identical. What changes is the service-partner fee: AED 55 when you file it yourself online, AED 95 when a trustee centre clerk files it for you, plus 5% VAT on that fee. That is the entire AED 42 difference — AED 219.75 against AED 177.75.

Is VAT charged on the Ejari fee?

Only on the service-partner portion. The AED 100 registration fee and the AED 10 knowledge and innovation fees are government charges outside the scope of VAT. The 5% applies to the partner fee alone — AED 2.75 online, AED 4.75 at a trustee centre.

Does Ejari renewal cost less than first registration?

No. Renewal costs exactly the same as a first registration — AED 177.75 online, AED 219.75 at a centre. There is no loyalty discount, and there is no surcharge for renewing late either; the fee is flat however overdue you are.

How much does it cost to cancel Ejari?

Nothing. Cancellation is free through Dubai REST or at a trustee centre. Because it costs nothing and an open registration on a unit blocks the next tenant's filing, there is no reason to skip it when you move out.

Who pays the Ejari fee — landlord or tenant?

UAE law does not assign it. Law No. 26 of 2007 places the registration duty on the landlord, but Dubai contracts routinely pass it to the tenant and in practice the tenant usually pays. Whichever way you agree, write it into the contract so it cannot be reopened later.

Is AED 300 or AED 500 for Ejari a scam?

Not necessarily a scam, but it is not the government fee. Agents, property managers and online filing services charge the AED 177.75 fee plus their own margin, and they are entitled to. What is not acceptable is presenting the total as though it were the official price. Ask any provider to show the government fee and their service fee as separate lines.

What else do I have to pay when moving into a Dubai rental?

Ejari is one of the smaller line items. Budget also for the agency commission (typically 5% of annual rent), the security deposit (5% unfurnished, 10% furnished), the DEWA connection deposit (AED 2,000 for an apartment, AED 4,000 for a villa) and the DEWA activation charges. Ejari at AED 177.75 is rarely what breaks the budget.
