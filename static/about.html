About Ejari Helper

Updated August 2026 · Independent service · Not affiliated with DLD or RERA

Ejari Helper is an independent Dubai tenancy toolkit: it fills the official DLD tenancy contract from your documents, answers rental-law questions grounded in the governing legislation, and publishes free guides with the actual fees rather than approximations. It is not a government service, not a filing agent, and it says so on every page.

How the guidance is sourced

Three instruments govern Dubai tenancies, and everything on this site is grounded in them:
Law No. 26 of 2007The tenancy law itself — registration duty, landlord and tenant obligations
Law No. 33 of 2008Amends Law 26/2007 — notice periods, eviction grounds, maintenance
Decree No. 43 of 2013The rent-increase caps, applied through the RERA rental index
The AI rental lawyer cites the article it is relying on, so you can check the source rather than take the answer on trust. It is built to say when something needs a human lawyer instead of guessing.
Fees and procedures are checked against the Dubai Land Department’s published rates and restated with the date they were verified. When a figure changes, the guides change — the date under each heading tells you when that page was last checked.

Frequently asked questions

Is Ejari Helper affiliated with the Dubai Land Department?

No. Ejari Helper is an independent service and has no affiliation with, endorsement from, or connection to the Dubai Land Department, RERA or any UAE government body. Ejari is a Dubai Land Department mark. Registration always happens through official channels — the Dubai REST app, dubailand.gov.ae, or a trustee centre.

Does Ejari Helper register Ejari for me?

No, and that is deliberate. We prepare the document that gets registered — the DLD Unified Tenancy Contract, auto-filled from your Emirates IDs and Title Deed — and we answer Dubai tenancy-law questions. You file the registration yourself for the government fee of AED 177.75, rather than paying us a markup to press the same buttons.

How is the legal guidance sourced?

Every answer is grounded in the three instruments that actually govern Dubai tenancies: Law No. 26 of 2007, Law No. 33 of 2008 which amends it, and Decree No. 43 of 2013 on rent increases. The AI rental lawyer cites the article it is relying on so you can check it, and it is built to say when a question needs a human lawyer.

What happens to the documents I upload?

Documents are used to extract the fields needed to fill your contract and are not sold or shared with third parties. The full detail is in the privacy policy, including retention. If you would rather not upload anything, the blank DLD template is free to download and fill by hand.

Is this legal advice?

No. The guides and the AI rental lawyer provide legal information, not legal advice, and no lawyer-client relationship is created. For a binding opinion, litigation, or anything where a large sum turns on the answer, instruct a UAE-licensed lawyer. The terms of service set this out in full.
