How to make a tenancy contract in Dubai

Official DLD format required · Contract free–AED 15 · Ejari registration AED 122.50

How do you make a tenancy contract in Dubai? Use the official DLD Unified Tenancy Contract form — Ejari rejects any other format — fill in the lessor, tenant, property and rent details, sign both parties, then register it with Ejari for AED 122.50. You can fill the form three ways: download the free template and type it yourself, let AI auto-fill it from your documents for AED 15, or pay a typing centre AED 100–200.

The three ways to make the contract

Fill the template yourself
Download the official form from our tenancy contract guide (Word or PDF) and type every field manually. Free, but field mistakes are the #1 cause of Ejari rejections — double-check the DEWA premise number and ID digits.

After the contract: Ejari registration

The signed contract is not the finish line — Dubai law requires it to be registered with Ejari before DEWA, visas or dispute filings will work. Registration is done in the Dubai REST app for AED 122.50 and takes about 10 minutes; the certificate usually arrives the same day.

For the step-by-step Ejari registration process after the contract is signed, see our Ejari registration guide. For renewals, see the Ejari renewal guide.
