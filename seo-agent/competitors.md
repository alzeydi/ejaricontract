# Competitor SERP notes

Refresh ~6 clusters per run (see AGENT.md §2). WebSearch runs on a US backend — order is approximate.
Last full sweep: **2026-10-23**.

## Who dominates the niche
* **MyBayut** — everywhere, very fresh (Oct 2026 updates on renewal, cancellation, DEWA, tenancy contract). Fees in bullets, “Process, Fees & More” titles, often no official links.
* **Gulf News** — owns Q&A/news intents (“who pays”, “who cancels”, deposits, 12-month notice, RDC stats).
* **Property Finder** — mid-2026 updates, “(2026 Guide)” titles, vague numbers (“nominal fee”, “~AED 220”).
* Then dubizzle (often stale 2025), bhomes (duplicate URLs, “2025 update” titles), fäm, Khaleej Times, Engel & Völkers, binghatti.
* New 2026 challengers: **realestateclubdubai.com** (2026 titles, FAQs, RDC fee calculator — but publishes unsourced “new law” claims) and **egsh.ae**.
* Official pages ranking: DLD Ejari templates + direct PDF (download intent), DEWA Move-in (#1 for “dewa move in”), rdc.gov.ae “track your case”.
* No ejari-branded competitor sites (ejari.org/.app/.support) and **no tools** in any top-10 — all guides/portals/news.

## Exploitable weaknesses
1. Stale years in competitor titles (fäm “2024”, bhomes “2025 update”, Bayut “in 2025”, PF calculator heading “2025”).
2. Conflicting fees across competitors for the same Ejari fee: 155 / 177.75 / 215 / ~220 / 219.75. Nobody but REC gives the RDC fee.
3. Official links rarely linked prominently.
4. “Calculator” and “template” queries won by pages that have neither — we have a real rent calculator, a downloadable contract and a contract generator.
5. Vague answers: no digit count/format for DEWA premise number or Ejari number; no honest “is there an Ejari fine” page.
6. Bayut states tenants can leave early on 90 days’ notice without penalty — wrong (Art. 7). Our early-termination page corrects it.
